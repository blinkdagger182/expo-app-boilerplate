───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1: BUILD FOR iOS (10-15 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

cd ~/Developer/document-ai/expo-app-boilerplate
eas build --platform ios --profile production

When prompted:
  • "Generate Apple Distribution Certificate?" → Press Y
  • "Generate Apple Provisioning Profile?" → Press Y

You'll get a URL to track progress. Wait for build to complete.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2: SUBMIT TO TESTFLIGHT (after build completes)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

eas submit --platform ios --latest

You'll need:
  • Apple ID: Your developer account email
  • App-Specific Password: Generate at appleid.apple.com
    (Sign-In & Security → App-Specific Passwords)
